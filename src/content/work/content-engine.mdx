The platform slowed down every time it succeeded — more content meant slower builds and
flakier pages. I moved the whole thing toward pre-rendering with a clear caching contract,
so growth became cheap instead of threatening.

The satisfying outcome was invisible: nobody talked about performance anymore, because it
stopped being a problem worth talking about.
