The starting point was a dense internal tool that showed everything and communicated
nothing. The work was less about adding features and more about deciding what deserved
attention during the worst ten minutes of someone's week.

I reframed the console around a small vocabulary of states, a strict typographic
hierarchy, and streaming updates that never moved content under the reader's eyes.
